# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated:** October 31, 2026

**Fifty Fifty Magazine** is built on a modern, privacy-conscious static web architecture. We maintain an ultra-minimal footprint and do not deploy aggressive cross-site tracking or third-party advertising cookies.

---

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text fragments stored on your local browser by websites you visit. They assist in remembering user preferences and ensuring core technical functionality.

---

## 2. Cookies We Use

Our publication relies strictly on essential, functional cookies:

* **Theme Preference (`theme`):** Remembers whether you prefer Dark Mode or Light Mode for comfortable reading across sessions.
* **Security & CDN Performance (`__cf_bm`, `cf_clearance`):** Utilized by Cloudflare to mitigate malicious bots, defend against automated spam, and deliver static assets at high speed globally.

---

## 3. Managing Cookies

You have the option to disable cookies entirely through your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Please note that while articles remain fully readable, certain preferences (such as dark mode persistence) may reset on page refresh.

For any inquiries regarding our cookie standards, contact: `privacy@fiftyfiftymag.com`.
