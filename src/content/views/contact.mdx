# Contact the Editorial Desk

We welcome tips, field corrections, route updates, and editorial inquiries from travelers, contributors, and independent researchers worldwide.

---

## A Distributed Cloud Newsroom

**Fifty Fifty Magazine** operates as a **100% cloud-native, globally distributed digital newsroom**. With our editors, field scouts, and contributors collaborating remotely across multiple continents, all editorial operations and inquiries are coordinated centrally through our digital desks.

---

## Editorial Inquiries & Story Pitches

Have on-the-ground news, route condition updates, or an honest dispatch from your travels? Reach out directly to our editorial team:

* **Editorial Desk:** `editorial@fiftyfiftymag.com`
* **General Correspondence:** `contact@fiftyfiftymag.com`
* **Corrections & Fact-Checking:** `desk@fiftyfiftymag.com`

*We aim to respond to all editorial communications within 24 to 48 business hours.*

---

## Community Field Reports

If you recently experienced an ongoing scam, sudden road closure, or pricing surge on any route we cover, please email our field desk with date and location details. Your anonymous field reports help keep thousands of independent travelers safe and properly informed.
