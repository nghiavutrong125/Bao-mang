# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 31, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 31, 2026

At **Fifty Fifty Magazine** (`https://50fiftymagazine.com`), we prioritize the privacy and security of our readers. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is used, and the steps we take to protect your personal data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Non-Personally Identifiable Information
When you browse Fifty Fifty Magazine, we may collect standardized, anonymous technical data via our content delivery network (Cloudflare), including:
* Browser type and version
* Operating system and device category
* Pages visited and timestamp
* Anonymous referral URLs

### B. Information You Voluntarily Provide
If you contact our editorial desk via email, we store your contact address and message content strictly for the purpose of corresponding with you. We never sell or rent your contact details.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We utilize anonymous log data solely to:
* Ensure server security, prevent DDoS attacks, and optimize page load speeds globally.
* Analyze reader interest in specific travel routes to guide editorial planning.
* Comply with applicable international data regulations.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & External Links

Our publication includes contextual links to external travel resources, transit schedules, and tour operators. Once you navigate away from Fifty Fifty Magazine, the privacy practices of those third-party sites apply. We encourage readers to review the privacy policies of any external destination.

---

## 4. Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal communications submitted to our desk. Inquiries regarding data privacy can be directed to: `info@50fiftymagazine.com`.
