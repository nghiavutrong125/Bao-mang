# Editorial Writing Skill - Fifty Fifty Magazine

Use this skill whenever drafting, editing, or formatting travel articles for Fifty Fifty Magazine.

## Core Writing Principles
1. **Natural Journalistic Style:** Write in fluent, sophisticated, and engaging English. Keep headings and body copy authentic, grounded, and free of gimmicks.
2. **NO Forced "50/50" Jargon:** 
   - Never insert the words "The 50/50", "50/50 Breakdown", or percentages ("50% Good vs 50% Bad") into article titles, headers, or body text.
   - Balance is maintained through substantive journalism: genuinely discussing both the stunning highlights and the honest drawbacks (e.g. cold weather, scams, slippery roads) without explicitly labeling them as "50/50".
3. **Structured Content Flow:**
   - **Lead Hook:** Date, location, and the current on-the-ground atmosphere.
   - **Overview / Highlights vs. Challenges:** Honest summary of what travelers face.
   - **Weather & Seasonality Table:** Clear temperatures, rain, and road visibility.
   - **Cultural & Botanical Context:** What is blooming, harvesting, or celebrating.
   - **Practical Field Advice:** Timing windows (e.g. 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM), clothing, bike safety, and local etiquette.
   - **Summary Metadata:** SEO Tags, coordinates, and culinary highlights.
4. **Media Standards:**
   - Always verify images are processed via `tools/image_pipeline.py` (stripped of EXIF, WebP compressed, hosted on Cloudflare R2 bucket `bao-mang`).
